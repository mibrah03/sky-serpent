Hop from planet to planet

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Sky Serpent ("the game") is made by the Sky Serpent team ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information stored on your device

Your coins, lives, helmets and settings are saved on your device, and uninstalling the game deletes them. Your level wins are also saved to our server, as described under "Cloud save and the Sky Serpent Challenge" below.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment card or billing details. To deliver and restore purchases, the game uses RevenueCat, a purchase service, which receives your purchase history and a random anonymous ID created by the app. RevenueCat encrypts this data in transit. RevenueCat's privacy policy.

How data is protected

Data is sent only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The game has no passwords and does not store payment details anywhere.

How long data is kept

Progress on your device stays until you uninstall the game. Cloud-saved level wins are kept until you delete them or ask us to. RevenueCat keeps purchase records so your purchases can be restored, until you ask for them to be deleted.

Cloud save and the Sky Serpent Challenge

The game saves your level progress to our server (Google Firebase) under a random anonymous ID, so that only real climbs count in challenges. We record which levels you completed, when, how long each took, your stars and your number of attempts. This is used to run the game and to prevent cheating.

If you complete all 200 levels during a challenge and choose to claim a prize, we collect your name, email address and state, and your confirmation that you are 18 or older and agree to the official rules. We use them only to verify your win and send your prize, and to email you about future challenges only if you opt in. We do not sell or share this information. We delete prize entries 12 months after a challenge ends, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, the game schedules reminders (lives refilled, helmet repaired, daily reward) on your device itself. Nothing is sent to a server, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings or your phone's settings.

What the game does not collect

Apart from a challenge prize claim, the game does not ask for your name or email. It never asks for contacts, photos or location, has no chat, and does not show ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

In the game: Settings > Delete my data removes your cloud save and any prize claim right away.

Without the app: email us with the subject "Delete my Sky Serpent data". We will delete your cloud save, prize claim and the purchase record linked to your anonymous ID within 30 days. Uninstalling the game deletes the progress stored on your phone.
